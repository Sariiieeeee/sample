Our Animals Contact Us

Jay and I grew up in the lowcountry and began keeping reptiles in 2022, starting with our first boa and royal python. Since then, we have slowly grown our collection and dipped our toes into breeding. Our first adventure was truly a rookie mistake when we housed together what we though were two female crested geckos. Before we knew it, and even after separating the pair, we were already on the path to hatching 12 babies. We learned a lot, which eventually lead us to pairing our corn snakes, Oreo and Peach, last fall. Down to our last baby, we are exciting about their next pairing and all the new experiences that are sure to come.
